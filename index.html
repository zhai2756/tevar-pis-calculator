    💡 About this model
    • Model: Ridge logistic regression (L2 penalty, C=0.667, lbfgs) with 9 predictors, including a squared neutrophil term
    • Development cohort: Northern Jiangsu People's Hospital (training n=540; 10-fold cross-validation AUC 0.806; training AUC 0.839)
    • Validation: internal n=136, AUC 0.784 (95% CI 0.684–0.884); external n=198 (The Second Affiliated Hospital of Nanjing Medical University), AUC 0.801 (95% CI 0.725–0.877), Brier 0.097
    • Threshold 0.321 (Youden-optimal, derived from development data): sensitivity 58.3%, specificity 94.6%, PPV 70.0%, NPV 91.4% (internal validation)
    • Inputs outside the clinical bounds used in model training are clipped before calculation (age 18–100 y; operation time 0–600 min; contrast 0–500 mL; neutrophils 0.5–40; APTT 15–100; fibrinogen 0.5–15; platelets 20–800)
    • This tool is for research use only and does not replace clinical judgment
